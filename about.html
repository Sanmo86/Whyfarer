About Whyfarer

A magazine built around one simple question: why?

Whyfarer started from a simple frustration: most lists of "interesting facts about other cultures" stop right at the fact, without ever explaining where it actually comes from. We wanted something different — a magazine where every custom gets a real answer, not just a trivia bullet point.

Each article is researched from publicly available historical, anthropological, and cultural sources, then written to read like a magazine feature rather than an encyclopedia entry — because understanding why a tradition exists is a lot more interesting than just knowing that it does.

Whyfarer publishes in English and Spanish, with every article mirrored in both languages, and runs as a small, independent, static website: no ad-tech bloat, no tracking beyond what's disclosed in our Privacy Policy, no clickbait.

All photography is real, sourced from Creative Commons contributors, and credited individually on our Photo Credits page.

Got a correction, a story idea, or a custom we should cover? See Contact — we read every email.
